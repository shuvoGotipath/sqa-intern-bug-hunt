Challenge rules & instructions

  1. Your mission: find defects in the ShopNest demo store — functional bugs, validation problems, boundary errors, confusing UI, incorrect messages and layout issues on small screens.
  2. Covered areas: registration & login, profile, product listing & search, cart, checkout and responsive layout.
  3. Report every issue with a clear title, the affected module, numbered steps to reproduce, expected result, actual result, severity and priority. Screenshots are optional but welcome.
  4. Quality over quantity. Reports are evaluated on correctness, reproducibility and clarity. Duplicate or vague reports earn little or nothing, and reports that describe intended behaviour count against you.
  5. Hints are available but each one you open lowers your score a little.
  6. Time is tracked for context only — careful work matters more than speed.
  7. Work on your own. No coding, special tools or paid software are needed. Your browser's built-in features are fine.
  8. Your progress is saved automatically on this browser. You can edit or delete reports until you submit. After submitting, reports are locked.
  9. When you're done, open Review & submit. Submitting the challenge unlocks the application form — your application is only sent once you submit that form too.
Test data you can use: demo account demo@shopnest.test / Shop@1234. You can also register new demo accounts. Payment is simulated — use any 16-digit test card number such as 4242 4242 4242 4242. Nothing you enter in ShopNest is real.
